**What to build:** In the old dialog, while AI nutrition suggestion runs, the submit button is disabled and the dialog can't be closed. ## Comments

### Triage, 2026-11-18

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

Decisions made with the owner:

- Leaving is never held. The AppBar back arrow and the browser's back button work during a suggestion, and the suggestion's result is ignored once the form is gone. Nothing is aborted.
- Cancelling pending requests on back across the whole app was considered and not taken. The gain wasn't visible, and aborting a query that the destination screen is still loading could leave that screen showing a load error.
- The router's `useBlocker` is not deprecated as the flow uses it. In `@tanstack/react-router` 1.170.x only the legacy overloads (a bare blocker function, `blockerFn`/`condition`) carry `@deprecated`, and the object form with `shouldBlockFn` is current. Nothing to replace.
- `ProductForm`'s `onNutritionSuggestingChange` stays optional. The old flow (`features/manageNote`) is not touched.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Disable **Create** and **Save** on the multi-item flow's product forms while nutrition values are being suggested

**Current behavior:**
In the multi-item food logging flow, the product form disables its fields and suggest buttons while an AI nutrition suggestion runs, but the submit button under it stays active. The owner can submit the product before the suggested values land. The products page's `ProductInputDialog` already disables its submit during a suggestion. The flow's forms don't, because they don't listen to the form's suggestion state.

**Desired behavior:**
On the create form (from a search's `Create "<query>"` row), the recognition form, and the edit form, the submit button (**Create** or **Save**) is visibly disabled from the moment a suggestion starts until it succeeds or fails. Then it works again. The AppBar back arrow and the browser's back button are unaffected: they stay enabled and leave the form during a suggestion, as they do at any other time.

**Key interfaces:**
- `ProductForm`'s optional `onNutritionSuggestingChange(nutritionSuggesting: boolean)` prop already reports when a suggestion starts and ends. The flow's create and edit forms pass it and track the value.
- The flow's shared product form footer, which renders the submit button with its `submitting` loading state, takes a way to be disabled while a suggestion runs.
- The recognition screen renders the flow's create form, so it gets the behaviour from there.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [x] With a suggestion in flight, **Create** on the create form and on the recognition form is disabled
- [x] With a suggestion in flight, **Save** on the edit form is disabled
- [x] After the suggestion succeeds, the button is enabled and submitting saves the suggested values
- [x] After the suggestion fails, the button is enabled again
- [x] With a suggestion in flight, tapping the AppBar back arrow leaves the form
- [x] Tests drive this through the rendered UI against the MSW mock API, with the suggestion handler held so the in-flight state can be checked
- [x] In the spec's "Old dialog behaviours" table, the row "Saving and closing are blocked while nutrition values are being suggested" is replaced by two rows: saving is blocked while suggesting (**Kept**, Product create, edit and recognition), and closing is blocked while suggesting (**Dropped**: leaving drops the form anyway, and the suggestion is ignored)
- [x] The spec's "Product create, edit and recognition" section says **Create** and **Save** are disabled while nutrition values are being suggested, and leaving is not held

**Out of scope:**
- Holding, disabling or blocking the AppBar back arrow or the browser's back button
- Aborting the suggestion request, or any other pending request, on leaving
- Making `onNutritionSuggestingChange` required
- Any change to the old flow (`features/manageNote`: `NoteInputDialog`, `ImageUploadStep`) or to the products page's `ProductInputDialog`
- Replacing or changing the flow's `useBlocker` and the discard prompt
